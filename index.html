Flickerdrum

A full-field ganzflicker that listens to a drumming track and locks its flicker to a multiple of the drum rate.

Photosensitive seizure warning. This page produces intense full-screen flashing light. Do not use it if you have epilepsy, a history of photosensitive seizures, or any doubt about your neurological response to strobe light. Stop immediately if you feel unwell.
